# Dental inner pages r30 visual review

## Scope

Read the current layout avoid list and `refinement/Final/checks.json`. Reviewed all available r30 tiles for the five inner pages at 1440 px and 390 px (51 tiles total): Treatments, Care Team, First Visit, Patient Information, and Contact. The checks report revision 30, one H1 per page, and `scrollWidth` 15 px below the named viewport, consistent across these pages.

## Findings

- Contained photos on Care Team and Patient Information have consistent rounded corners at desktop and phone widths. The Treatments room image and First Visit reception image are full-width panoramas with square edges, which read as intentional edge-to-edge sections. Contact has no photo. No accidental mixed corner treatment observed.
- Page headings and supporting copy remain stacked. The patient-information consultation photo sits beside one heading, short explanation, and CTA at desktop; it does not form a narrow multi-item information rail. At phone width that content stacks cleanly.
- Patient Information copy reads as practical visitor guidance. Its revised FAQ has a plain-language explanation question and contains no appointment-confirmation filler. Contact guidance stays focused on the message and comfort/access needs, with no setup instructions.
- The Contact form is visibly disabled: its send button is muted, and the adjacent text states, “This form is not configured to receive submissions.” This disclosure remains clear at desktop and mobile widths.
- Sections, photos, accordions, form labels and buttons fit the reviewed desktop and phone tiles without visible clipping or overlap. No material visual defect found.

This was a screenshot review only. Accordion and form keyboard behavior and actual submission remain outside this review.
