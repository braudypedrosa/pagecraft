# Mossline Gardens design plan

## Direction

Mossline Gardens is a fictional landscape design studio with a quiet field-journal identity. Broad garden photography carries atmosphere; open ledgers and plain captions carry practical information. Deep pine, limestone, soft leaf and a muted seed-pink accent distinguish the brand without decorative counters, artificial statistics or generic luxury language.

The applied `ui-ux-pro-max` query was `landscape garden earthy natural` with variance 6, motion 2 and density 4. Its natural green palette, spacious Swiss structure, low motion and accessible contrast fit the brief. The source adapts those principles into a more editorial garden identity. The generated master record remains at `design-system/mossline-gardens/MASTER.md`.

## Page and section plan

- **Home:** split photographic hero; cohesive studio introduction; open service ledger; paired illustrative garden studies; process image with named phases; woodland photo CTA.
- **Services:** service scope ledger; process image and briefing guidance; top-aligned practical FAQ; woodland photo CTA.
- **Gardens:** explicit illustration disclosure; dominant meadow study; equal courtyard and terrace studies; path-and-rain-garden narrative; woodland photo CTA.
- **Contact:** quote introduction; unconfigured native form beside process image; plain preparation guide.
- **Shared header/footer:** graphic logo, native fullscreen mobile navigation, compact dark footer with one link group and a quiet demo notice.

## Native implementation

All content uses native Pagecraft nodes: sections, boxes, headings, images, buttons, navigation, accordion and form. Global colors and text styles are editable tokens. Header and footer are shared document regions. The source has no custom CSS, injected head markup or HTML replica.

Responsive behavior is authored at desktop, tablet and mobile breakpoints. Multi-column layouts collapse into a clear reading order; study pairs remain paired at tablet where space permits. Buttons are at least 44px high and keyboard focus styling uses a visible gold outline.
