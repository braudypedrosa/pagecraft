# Customizing Maren & Tomás · Lisbon 2027 (maren-and-tomas@1.0.0)

## Identity, Typography & Palette
- **Site Name & Metadata**: Open **Settings › General** to replace **Maren & Tomás · Lisbon 2027**, update the favicon (`asset:favicon`), and set your social preview image.
- **Typography**: Heading font and body font are governed globally via **Settings › Typography** without pinned heading-level font overrides.
- **Color Tokens**: Adjust brand, surface, ink, muted and border tokens under **Global Styles › Colors** to personalize the palette cleanly.

## Interactive Widgets & Sections
- Select any native Audio player, Countdown, Marquee, Tag Filter, Slider, Gallery, Table, Code block, or Video facade directly on the canvas to adjust settings in the Inspector.
