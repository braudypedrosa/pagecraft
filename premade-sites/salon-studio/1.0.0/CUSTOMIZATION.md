# Morrow & Muse customization guide

## Start with verified business details

Replace the fictional name, logo, team profiles and service descriptions with current owner-approved information. Add a real address, hours, access notes, parking or public transport details, phone number and social links only after verifying them. Do not publish the fictional names Mara Vale, Ellis Rowe or Noa Mercer as real staff.

## Service menu and pricing

The template intentionally omits prices. Replace each service row with the salon’s actual service name, scope, duration, starting price or price range, consultation rule and maintenance notes. State when final pricing depends on hair length, density, product use, corrective work or consultation. Confirm deposits, cancellation terms, late-arrival policy and group or event-styling terms.

## Appointment inquiry

The contact form uses `mode: external` with an empty action. It is inert by design.

Before launch:

1. Choose the approved recipient or booking provider.
2. Configure the supported Pagecraft form destination. Never paste a private secret into page content.
3. Test required fields, validation, keyboard use, error messages and successful delivery in the target host.
4. Explain the expected reply time and how an appointment becomes confirmed.
5. Add current privacy, deposit and cancellation information.
6. Keep the wording clear that an inquiry is not an appointment until the salon confirms a date, time, stylist and service.

## Team and claims

Replace every profile with the correct name, role, pronouns, service focus and current photograph. Add qualifications, certifications, product partnerships, awards, review excerpts, statistics or sustainability claims only when the owner can verify them and has permission to publish them. Add review source and attribution when real reviews are used.

## Photography

Replace demonstration imagery with current licensed salon photography. Keep the intended roles:

- `studio-interior`: wide architectural scene with room for the hero copy panel.
- `texture-craft`: close craft image focused on hands and hair texture.
- `studio-team`: natural environmental group portrait.

Set accurate alt text for each replacement. Avoid repeating one photograph across unrelated homepage sections. Verify crops at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px.

## Brand system

Edit global tokens first. Plum and berry control primary actions and dark surfaces; rose and blush provide section separation; paper and ink carry most reading content. Update global text styles rather than styling headings one at a time. Check normal body and control contrast after every palette change.

## Final checks

- Test every menu, logo, button and anchor link after import.
- Confirm fullscreen mobile navigation works without overflow.
- Replace long headings and check that the hero remains comfortably short.
- Verify image replacement, global styles, duplicate/reorder/delete, responsive controls, save/reopen and published output.
- Test the connected form without using a real customer message.
- Complete fresh import and reimport checks in both Pagecraft Cloud and WordPress before promotion.
