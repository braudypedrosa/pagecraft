# Vacation-rental homepage research — 2026-09-08

Primary sources inspected through web search and the built-in browser:

- https://sandandstoneescapes.com/ — visible date-search control, featured property imagery with sleeping capacity, collections grouped around guest needs, booking FAQs. Adopt property-bound facts and planning help; do not copy its offers, claims or catalog size.
- https://www.uniquehomestays.com/ — destination/date/guest search at the opening; recent properties with location and descriptive distinctions; collection discovery. Adopt direct paths to homes and decision-critical details. Two example homes do not justify a disconnected multi-destination search UI.
- https://www.thethinkingtraveller.com/ — destination/date/guest/bedroom search, destination browsing and specialist inquiry. Adopt a clear inquiry route and explain booking steps. Do not invent service credentials or availability.

Research conclusion: Salt House lacked guest-decision content. A homepage must explain what can be rented, help a group choose, and make the next booking step clear. The new homepage contains a property-photo opening, native home listings, a comparison table, coastal-setting information, an arrangement process and native FAQs. It removes the repeated closing image banner.

Native boundary: Pagecraft table, accordion, section, image, button and shared home component. No fake date search, live rates or submission success. Booking-provider integration remains out of scope. Prior GSAP/native-scroll-attribute importer limitation still applies.

Visual review: desktop and mobile renders in evidence/rental-homepage. Opening photo geometry fills both columns and both rows; mobile becomes a broad image above two smaller interior views. Heading final rendering is one line on mobile and two on desktop. This is an agent assessment, not user approval.

User feedback on preceding candidate: “Are we serious about this design?” and “The homepage is lacking.” Scope: Salt House homepage and its vacation-rental purpose. No global skill change inferred.
