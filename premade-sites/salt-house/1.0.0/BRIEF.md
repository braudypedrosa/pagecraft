# Salt House — vacation-rental collection

User scope: small vacation-rental collection, selected explicitly in this task. New template; do not replace Marea or architecture-studio.

Visual thesis: sun-bleached coastal homes, blue ink and clear, useful property information.
Content plan: brand and place; browse homes; individual stay details; local guide; practical planning; inquiry or booking-provider handoff.
Interaction thesis: native menu disclosure, restrained link hover/focus, and native image gallery/lightbox on property pages. Motion must remain editable and respect reduced motion; no ornamental JavaScript.

Proposed sitemap: Home, The Homes, Dune House, Blue Shutter Cottage, The Coast, Plan Your Stay / Contact. Native reusable property components and shared navigation/footer. No invented reviews, occupancy, prices, availability or operating credentials. All property details and imagery are clearly identified demonstration content.

Booking: link to the owner's configured booking provider. Until configured, use an honest inquiry/setup state. No fake calendar search or simulated booking success. Forms require a tested receiving endpoint.

Directions: Coastline (recommended): centered oversized brand, full-width photograph, paired property comparison; Guestbook: ochre editorial split with a sequential featured-home story; Collection: compact navy header and property comparison immediately after a concise introduction. These native sketches contain a hero and meaningful body section, with desktop/mobile compositions. They have not passed full installation or release QA.

Recent-history comparison: avoids Quiet Tide/Strandline's lower-left white serif overlay and booking rail, Morrow Field's rejected side dock, and Common Ground's framed image-before-serif-title opening. Familiar property facts and links remain consistent for usability.
