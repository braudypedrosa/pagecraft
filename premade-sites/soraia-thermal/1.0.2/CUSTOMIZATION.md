# Customizing SORAIA — Thermal Basalt Reserve (soraia-thermal@1.0.2)

## Identity, Typography & Palette
- **Site Name & Metadata**: Open **Settings › General** to replace **SORAIA — Thermal Basalt Reserve**, update the favicon (`asset:favicon`), and set your social preview image.
- **Typography**: Heading font and body font are governed globally via **Settings › Typography** without pinned heading-level font overrides.
- **Color Tokens**: Adjust brand, surface, ink, muted and border tokens under **Global Styles › Colors** to personalize the palette cleanly.
- **Floating Global Header**: Toggle **Float over hero** in the Header Section inspector to adjust overlay blending over `100dvh` hero bands.

## Interactive Widgets & Sections
- Select any native Image Hotspots, Image Compare slider, Timeline, Progress bars, Tag Filter, Tabs, Slider, Gallery, Audio player, Countdown, Marquee, Table, Code block, or Lightbox Video trigger directly on the canvas to configure in the Inspector.
