# Customize the Dental Clinic template

Before using this site for a real practice, replace the Linden Dental name, logo, colors and photographs with the practice's approved brand and imagery. Review the Home, Treatments, About and Contact pages and replace sample copy with accurate services, contact details and practice information.

The six doctor names, profiles and portraits are illustrative sample content. Replace them with the practice's real team details and approved photographs. Verify professional titles, specialties and biographies before publishing.

The Contact form has three required fields: name, email and message. Its external action is blank in the template. In Cloud, use the available managed form receiver and verify that the intended site owner can receive and handle enquiries. In WordPress, configure a suitable HTTPS form endpoint through the native form controls and verify delivery before publishing. Do not enter a destination until the practice has supplied and approved it.

The request-a-visit links lead to the Contact page. They do not provide an appointment booking engine or confirm appointments. If the practice uses an external booking provider, add its approved link and check that it works. Review all links, navigation and mobile layouts before launch.

In WordPress, open Theme Builder and choose Edit design to update the shared header or footer.
