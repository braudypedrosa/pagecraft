# Ledger & Loom customization guide

This package is a fictional bookkeeping-practice demo. Replace the brand, scope, and imagery with verified business information before publication.

## Brand and global styles

- Replace both logo assets together so light and dark surfaces retain the same mark.
- Update native color tokens rather than adding page-level color overrides.
- Update Outfit and Work Sans through Pagecraft global typography if a different licensed pairing is required.
- Keep body text at least 16px and preserve visible focus, 44px controls, and readable contrast.

## Services and claims

- Confirm which bookkeeping platforms, business types, payroll tasks, locations, and reporting cycles the real practice supports.
- Replace package names and scope descriptions with the real engagement model. Add prices only when the owner supplies current public pricing.
- Do not add tax, investment, legal, certification, compliance, or outcome claims without verified evidence and appropriate professional review.
- Add testimonials, client logos, statistics, team biographies, and credentials only from approved source material.

## Consultation form

The native Contact form is deliberately unconfigured and renders disabled until an authorized destination is supplied. In Pagecraft, select the form, choose the correct handling mode, add the approved HTTPS destination or WordPress connector, test delivery with authorized recipients, and confirm privacy/retention wording. Never paste a real recipient or endpoint into the source template.

## Pages, menu, and media

- Shared header and footer control sitewide navigation. Keep menu items page-backed when the host supports native menu binding.
- Replace each photograph by role: hero workspace, hands-on records process, and quiet studio/archive setting. Keep descriptive alt text tied to the visible replacement.
- Review all four pages at 390, 768, 1024, and 1440px after copy or image changes. Long business names and service headings need a specific overflow check.
