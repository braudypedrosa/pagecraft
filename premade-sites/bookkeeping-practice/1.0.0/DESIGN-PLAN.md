# Ledger & Loom design plan

Ledger & Loom uses a precision-editorial identity: near-black ink, warm ivory paper, restrained ochre, strict rules, geometric display type, and tactile bookkeeping photography. The homepage avoids a generic feature-card stack and moves through five different compositions.

1. **Hero — ordered worktable.** Full photographic field with an anchored ink reading panel. Sets the quiet, exact tone and gives two clear paths.
2. **Scope — ruled service records.** Keeps the introduction together, then uses substantial service rows for scanning without decorative counters.
3. **Workbench — image and practice note.** Connects the service to the real act of sorting records with one coherent image-copy composition.
4. **Monthly rhythm — dark process band.** Explains Collect, Reconcile, and Review without numbered steps or outcome claims.
5. **Fit and consultation — bounded scope, then photo CTA.** Clarifies what belongs with a bookkeeper before a concise contact invitation.

Inner pages keep the same system but change reading rhythm: Services uses long-form package bands, Approach uses a broad documentary image and process records, and Contact uses a focused form plus native accordion. Shared Pagecraft header and footer carry the brand, menu, mobile navigation, and demo disclosure across all pages.

All structure uses editable Pagecraft nodes, tokens, text styles, state styles, menus, forms, accordions, and shared header/footer regions. No custom CSS, head markup, JavaScript, or HTML replica is included.
