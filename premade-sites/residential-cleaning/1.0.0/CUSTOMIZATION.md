# Customization guide

1. Replace the fictional business name, logo, favicon, and all demonstration photography through Pagecraft media controls.
2. Update the service area, working hours, inclusions, exclusions, preparation guidance, and product policy from verified operating information.
3. Rename or remove the illustrative package labels. Add prices only when current pricing, taxes, minimums, and scope are confirmed.
4. Configure the quote form destination. Test delivery, errors, consent language, privacy handling, and the follow-up process before accepting inquiries.
5. Add real contact details only after the recipient and response process are ready. Do not imply instant booking unless a booking provider is configured and tested.
6. Replace demonstration claims with evidence-backed statements. Add reviews, ratings, insurance, screening, sustainability, or satisfaction claims only when verifiable and permitted.
7. Check every page at 390, 768, 1024, and 1440px after longer headings, new photos, or changed navigation labels.

Global colors and typography are native Pagecraft tokens. Header, footer, navigation, buttons, images, form fields, and responsive layout remain editable without custom CSS or head scripts.
