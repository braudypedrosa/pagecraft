# Asset provenance

All assets are bundled in `assets/`. Photography was generated with the built-in OpenAI image generator on 2026-10-13, inspected, then converted to 1600×1067 WebP at quality 84. No third-party source image, logo, visible brand or real person identity was supplied.

## Photography prompts

- `panel-hero.webp`: editorial documentary photograph of an electrician reviewing a modern residential electrical panel in a bright renovated home; wide landscape, subject on the right, negative space on the left; no text, branding, sparks, danger or license numbers.
- `rewire-detail.webp`: documentary close-medium photograph of gloved hands routing cable during a tasteful renovation; organized work surface, soft daylight; no text, branding, exposed live wires, sparks or unsafe practice.
- `lighting-room.webp`: architectural photograph of a modern living and dining room with layered warm lighting; wide balanced composition at early evening; no text, branding or visible product marks.
- `team-planning.webp`: candid photograph of two tradespeople in navy workwear discussing plans at a workshop table; collaborative daylight scene; no text, branding, badges or unsafe equipment use.

The images are illustrative. They must not be represented as Copperline staff, customer homes or completed projects.

## Logo

`logo.svg` and `logo-light.svg` are original vector assets authored for this template. The mark combines a horizontal switch line with a compact current trace. The light version preserves the same geometry for the ink footer.
