# Public form control dependencies

- `@zag-js/date-picker` 1.46.0 and `@zag-js/vanilla` 1.46.0 — MIT, [chakra-ui/zag](https://github.com/chakra-ui/zag). Bundled into the public date runtime.
- `@internationalized/date` 3.12.4 — Apache-2.0, [adobe/react-spectrum](https://github.com/adobe/react-spectrum/tree/main/packages/%40internationalized/date). Bundled through Zag.
- Calendar and chevron SVG paths — `lucide:calendar-days`, `lucide:chevron-left`, `lucide:chevron-right`, and `lucide:chevron-down` from [Iconify](https://iconify.design/), retrieved from `api.iconify.design` on 2026-10-20. Lucide is ISC licensed.

Exact dependency versions and integrity hashes are recorded in `package-lock.json`.
